## 2. Industry Understanding

Generator rental is a service business where customers purchase access to reliable electrical power for a specific duration rather than purchasing the generator itself.

Customers may need generators because:
- The local electricity supply is unavailable or unreliable.
- Their event requires a dependable power source.
- Their industrial operations cannot afford power interruptions.
- Their construction or project site lacks permanent electricity.
- They need temporary backup during maintenance or an emergency.

Customers are not simply renting a machine. They are seeking **continuity, reliability, convenience, and confidence that power will be available when it matters.**

The website must communicate these outcomes rather than presenting itself as a simple generator catalogue.

### Important Industry Considerations

1. Power requirements vary according to equipment, electrical loads, operating conditions, and simultaneous usage.
2. Generator capacity must be selected according to the customer's actual requirements.
3. Event customers may prioritize quiet operation, guest experience, dependable supply, and timely coordination.
4. Industrial customers may prioritize capacity, operational continuity, equipment compatibility, and service coordination.
5. Rental duration, transportation, installation, cabling, fuel, operator availability, and other service inclusions must be confirmed with the business.
6. Availability and delivery commitments must never be promised without verification.

5 kVA–250 kVA.

### Main Application Categories
1. Wedding and marriage functions
2. Grand events and celebrations
3. Stage, sound, and lighting requirements
4. Industrial projects
5. Construction and temporary project sites
6. Commercial backup power requirements, subject to service availability

The additional applications above describe potential use cases, not verified contractual service commitments. Confirm the actual service offering with the business.

### Capacity Selection Principles

Do not automatically assign a generator capacity to a customer based only on their industry.

The required capacity may depend on:
- Total connected electrical load.
- Starting current of motors and other equipment.
- Single-phase or three-phase requirements.
- Power factor and operating conditions.
- Simultaneous equipment usage.
- Required reserve capacity.
- Site conditions and the recommendations of a qualified professional.

The website may explain the available 5 kVA–250 kVA range and invite customers to request assistance selecting a suitable generator.

Do not publish unsupported capacity-to-application mappings or guarantee suitability without assessing the actual load.

### Silent Generator Positioning

Communicate the benefits of silent or low-noise generator solutions while avoiding absolute claims such as “zero noise.”

Do not publish unverified decibel levels, emissions figures, fuel-consumption figures, or performance guarantees.

## 8. Design Direction

### Overall Visual Identity

The website should feel like a premium, modern power-solutions company rather than a generic equipment listing website.

The visual experience should balance industrial confidence with clean, approachable service design.

### Color System: Energy Orange

Follow the 60/30/10 visual distribution principle.

**60% — Soft Ivory**
- HEX: #FFF7EB
- Use for main backgrounds, content surfaces, and spacious sections.

**30% — Charcoal**
- HEX: #2B2B2B
- Use for headings, primary text, navigation, strong structural elements, and selected dark sections.

**10% — Power Orange**
- HEX: #FF8A00
- Use for primary calls to action, active states, important icons, and selected highlights.

Use dark text on orange surfaces wherever needed to maintain readable contrast.

Avoid saturating the entire interface with orange. The color should emphasize important actions and brand moments.

### Typography Direction

Desired characteristics:
- Slim
- Unique
- Premium
- Professional
- Highly readable
- Appropriate for an industrial service business

Manrope is the leading initial candidate for testing, with Space Grotesk as a more distinctive alternative.

The final typeface should be selected after visual comparison and responsive testing. Do not treat a candidate as finalized until approved.

Use restrained typography, controlled line lengths, fluid heading sizes, and consistent font weights.

### Visual References and Inspiration

Use Awwwards-level design quality as a reference for:
- Typography hierarchy
- Spacing and composition
- High-quality imagery
- Refined animation
- Responsive layouts
- Interaction details

Do not copy another website's layout or visual identity directly.

### Imagery Direction

Prioritize realistic, high-quality imagery of:
- Silent generator equipment
- Wedding venues and event installations
- Stage and event production
- Industrial and construction environments
- Professional equipment deployment

Actual company equipment photographs should be preferred when available.

Avoid irrelevant stock photographs, fake project imagery, misleading equipment specifications, and visuals that do not represent the real business.

## 11. Rental Enquiry Journey

Recommended customer journey:

1. **Discover:** The visitor finds the website through search, referrals, or advertising.
2. **Identify:** The visitor selects weddings, events, industrial projects, or another supported requirement.
3. **Explore:** The visitor reviews relevant solutions and the available generator capacity range.
4. **Enquire:** The visitor submits their location, date, duration, and requirements.
5. **Assess:** The business confirms the load requirements, equipment availability, logistics, and service inclusions.
6. **Quote:** The business provides a quotation based on the confirmed requirements.
7. **Confirm:** The customer confirms the booking according to the business's terms.
8. **Deliver:** The rental service is fulfilled according to the agreed arrangement.

The website must not imply that a form submission automatically confirms a reservation.
